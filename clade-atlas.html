    How to read it

    1. Inside means descended from. Every region sits entirely within its ancestors' regions.
    2. Thick borders are old splits. Line weight scales with divergence time, so crossing a heavy line means a distant relative.
    3. Neighbouring hues are relatives. Each clade gets a slice of the colour wheel, and its subclades divide that slice.

    Simplified tree of 55 animal groups. Dates are rounded estimates in millions of years.
